TOPIC 09 — GROUP BY AND HAVING

Purpose
GROUP BY creates groups so aggregate functions can calculate values per group. HAVING filters those groups after aggregation.

Why LEFT JOIN?
It allows departments with no employees to remain in the joined result. The HAVING condition in this example then shows only departments with at least one employee.

WHERE versus HAVING
- WHERE filters individual rows before grouping.
- HAVING filters groups after aggregation.

Try it
Remove the HAVING clause and observe what changes if you add an empty department.
