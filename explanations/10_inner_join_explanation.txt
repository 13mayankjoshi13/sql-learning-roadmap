TOPIC 10 — INNER JOIN

Purpose
JOIN combines related rows from two tables.

INNER JOIN returns rows where the join condition matches on both sides. Here, an employee appears only if their department ID matches a department row.

Aliases
`employees AS e` and `departments AS d` shorten table names and make queries easier to read.

Try it
Add `d.department_name = 'Engineering'` to the WHERE clause.
