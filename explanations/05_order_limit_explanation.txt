TOPIC 05 — ORDER BY AND LIMIT

Purpose
ORDER BY sorts results; LIMIT restricts the number of rows returned.

Key points
- ASC sorts from low to high or oldest to newest.
- DESC sorts from high to low or newest to oldest.
- LIMIT 2 returns at most two rows.
- If ties matter, add a second sort column to make ordering predictable.

Expected result
The first query returns the two highest-paid employees. The second sorts employees by hiring date.

Try it
Show the single earliest-hired employee.
